# SEO / Google Ads landing-page pass — 2026-10-27

Why: Google Ads rated the landing-page experience "below average" on nearly every keyword. The
searched spelling of each ad keyword (no hamza: «ارضيات», «باركيه ارضيات») now appears verbatim in
the `<title>`, the H1, the first paragraph and the meta description of its landing page.

**Title lock: a changed title stays unchanged for 21 days (until 2026-11-17) so Google can settle
on it.** Log every later title change here with its date.

How titles are built:

- Product pages: the part before « - » in `title` (`data/products.ts`) is the display name and the
  H1. `app/products/[id]/page.tsx` builds `<title>` as `<name> في الرياض` and the layout template
  appends ` | موكيت ومفروشات السريع` (brand once). When that would exceed 60 characters the city is
  dropped from the `<title>` (it stays in the description).
- Hub pages: `categoryContent[slug].title` in `lib/products.ts`, same template.
