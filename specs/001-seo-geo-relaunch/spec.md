# Feature Specification: SEO + GEO relaunch of mokeet-elsuarye.com

**Feature Branch**: `001-seo-geo-relaunch`
**Created**: 2026-10-27
**Status**: Draft
**Input**: Owner (2026-10-27): «حطلي خطة وبلان لإعادة تحسين وهيكلة السيو والجيو … خطة كاملة لرفع الموقع زي ما تم رفع وتحسين مفروشات السريع، والبداية تكون من تغيير جميع اللوجوهات والكارات وأشكالها لتكون احترافية زي موقع همسات وفنون العاديات. اللوجوهات الحالية معمولة بالـ AI، عايز لوجوهات SVG وصور حقيقية.»

## Context and baseline

The site sells flooring from a showroom in Haraj Bin Qasim, Riyadh, and is the landing site of a Google Ads account
that spends about 200 SAR a day. Google rates its landing-page experience «below average» on nearly every ad keyword,
and organic search brings almost nothing.

| Measure (Google Search Console) | mokeet-elsuarye.com | mafrushat-alsurye.com (the sister site, after its rescue) |
|---|---|---|
| Window | 90 days to 2026-10-26 | 28 days to 2026-10-26 |
| Clicks | 8 | 47 |
| Impressions | ~530 | 3,101 |
| Average position | ~31 (home page) | 15 |
| Pages with impressions | home + 10 thin URLs, most of them old category URLs now redirected | most product pages |

What the owner sees today and wants replaced:

- **Icons**: every small symbol on the site (sector tiles, offer cards, steps, contact rows, map/clock/phone) is a
  generic library glyph in a coloured square. They read as generic and «AI-made» next to the sister sites.
- **Brand logo**: the «السريع» badge is a raster picture generated by an AI tool (it carried a Gemini watermark
  before it was cropped). There is no vector version, so it blurs when scaled and cannot be recoloured.
- **Photos**: 8 products still show licensed stock photos labelled «صورة توضيحية». The owner has sent real
  photos for mosques, gym rubber and office tiles (live since 2026-10-27); parquet, water-resistant parquet, medical
  vinyl, home carpet, Turkish carpet and artificial grass still lack them.
- **Cards**: section cards are plain text-in-a-box; the reference sites (hamasat-beity, funun-aladiyat) use photo-led
  cards, layered compositions and a consistent icon family.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - A visitor from an ad trusts the shop at first glance (Priority: P1)

A facility manager taps a Google ad for «ربل جيم» on a phone. The page they land on looks like an established
showroom: a crisp brand mark, a consistent set of purpose-drawn icons, cards that show the shop's own installed
floors, and the call/WhatsApp buttons on the first screen. They message the shop.

**Why this priority**: Every paid visit lands here. A page that looks generic loses the contact and lowers Google's
landing-page rating, which raises the price of every click.

**Independent Test**: Open the five ad landing pages (home, parquet hub, rubber hub, medical vinyl, brand) on a
390 px phone and a desktop; every icon comes from one family, the logo is sharp at every size, and every product
card shows a real photo or none.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** any page, **When** it is viewed at 1x, 2x and 3x screen density, **Then** the brand mark and all icons
   render sharp with no visible pixels or compression artefacts.
2. **Given** any icon on the site, **When** it is compared with the others, **Then** stroke width, corner style,
   size grid and colour rules match across the whole set.
3. **Given** a product that has owner photos, **When** its card or page is shown, **Then** an owner photo leads and
   no stock photo appears before it.
4. **Given** a product with no owner photo yet, **When** its card is shown, **Then** the stock photo keeps its
   «صورة توضيحية» label until the owner's photo replaces it.

---

### User Story 2 - Google ranks the site for what Riyadh searches (Priority: P1)

Someone searches «باركيه الرياض», «ارضيات مطاط», «موكيت مساجد» or the brand «موكيت السريع». The site appears on
page one with a title and description that match the search, and branded searches show the site first.

**Why this priority**: Organic visits are free; the sister site went from near zero to 47 clicks a month with the
same playbook.

**Independent Test**: Search Console shows impressions for each priority page and rising positions for the keyword
clusters below, measured at 2, 4 and 8 weeks after launch.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the brand searches («موكيت السريع», «السريع للموكيت», «مفروشات السريع الرياض»), **When** they are
   searched in Riyadh, **Then** the site is in the top 3 within 4 weeks.
2. **Given** each product cluster, **When** its landing page is crawled, **Then** the page's title, main heading and
   first paragraph use the market's own spelling of the term, and the page has enough unique content to stand alone.
3. **Given** the old URLs Google still reports, **When** they are requested, **Then** each one permanently redirects
   to its live equivalent and none returns «not found».

---

### User Story 3 - AI assistants recommend the shop (GEO) (Priority: P2)

Someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI overview «وين ألاقي ربل جيم في الرياض؟» or «محل موكيت مساجد في حراج
بن قاسم». The answer names «موكيت ومفروشات السريع» with correct facts (address, phone, products) and links to it.

**Why this priority**: A growing share of buyers ask assistants before they search. Assistants quote pages that
state facts plainly, consistently and in question-and-answer form.

**Independent Test**: A fixed set of 20 Arabic prompts is run monthly in at least two assistants; the share that
mention or cite the shop is recorded.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the 20 tracked prompts, **When** they are asked 8 weeks after launch, **Then** the shop is mentioned
   in at least 4 answers (from 0 at baseline, to be measured before launch).
2. **Given** any public fact about the business, **When** it appears on the site, in structured data and in the
   Google Business Profile, **Then** it is identical in all three.

---

### User Story 4 - The owner can update photos and offers without a developer (Priority: P3)

The owner sends new photos or a dated offer on WhatsApp; they go live the same day through one place in the code,
with no layout work.

**Independent Test**: Adding a photo folder or an offer entry changes the live page after one deploy with no other
edit.

### Edge Cases

- A product has only one owner photo: the card and gallery still look complete (no empty thumbnails).
- An owner photo shows a person's face or another business's sign: it is not used.
- An owner photo is byte-identical to one on another client's site: it is not used.
- The brand-mark redraw does not match the badge closely enough for the owner: the current badge stays until he
  approves a version.
- A visitor has reduced motion turned on: icons and cards appear without animation.
- A page has fewer cards than a full row: the row stays centred and balanced.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Phase A — Brand, icons, cards and photos (first, per the owner)**

- **FR-001**: The brand mark («السريع» badge: S emblem with the wordmark) MUST exist as a vector file faithful to
  the current badge, in a full-colour version, a one-colour version for dark backgrounds, and a square emblem-only
  version for small sizes; the owner approves it before it replaces anything.
- **FR-002**: Site icons, favicon, home-screen icon and the logo that search engines read MUST be generated from
  that vector mark.
- **FR-003**: Every interface icon MUST come from one coherent family (one stroke weight, one corner style, one
  size grid), drawn or chosen for this business: sectors (mosque, office, gym, hospital, home), services
  (inspection and measurement, sample, supply and installation, delivery), contact (phone, WhatsApp, location,
  hours), navigation (arrows, chevrons, menu, close) and product types (carpet roll, carpet tile, parquet plank,
  rubber roll, vinyl).
- **FR-004**: Section cards (sectors, offers, how-we-work, contact rows, product cards) MUST be redesigned in the
  manner of hamasat-beity and funun-aladiyat: photo-led where a real photo exists, layered compositions, consistent
  radius, border and shadow rules, and the approved mint/ink/brass palette. Mockups are approved by the owner before
  build.
- **FR-005**: Every product MUST show owner photos first when they exist; stock photos stay labelled «صورة توضيحية»
  and are listed in a «photos still needed» register that the owner can see.
- **FR-006**: No photo of another client, no watermarked photo and no photo showing a recognisable face may be
  used; every new photo is checked for byte-identical copies in the other client sites before it goes live.

**Phase B — Technical SEO**

- **FR-007**: Every old URL that Search Console still reports MUST redirect permanently to its live equivalent.
- **FR-008**: Every page MUST have one main heading, a self-referencing canonical address, a unique title and
  description, and appear in the sitemap with its real last-change date.
- **FR-009**: Mobile pages MUST meet Google's «good» thresholds for loading, interactivity and layout stability for
  real visitors (field data), measured per landing page.
- **FR-010**: Structured data MUST describe the business, its products/services, breadcrumbs and FAQs, using only
  facts the owner confirmed, and validate without errors.

**Phase C — Content and keyword architecture**

- **FR-011**: Each keyword cluster MUST have exactly one target page; overlapping pages are merged or redirected.
  Clusters: brand; parquet (باركيه ارضيات، باركيه الرياض، باركيه SPC، باركيه ضد الماء، تركيب باركيه); rubber
  (ارضيات مطاط، ربل جيم، ربل حمامات، بلاط مطاط، فوم اطفال); medical vinyl (فينيل طبي، ارضيات المستشفيات); vinyl
  look (مشمع باركيه، بديل الباركيه); mosque (موكيت مساجد، سجاد مساجد); office (موكيت مكاتب، بلاط موكيت); home
  carpet (موكيت غرف ومجالس، موكيت تركي).
- **FR-012**: Each target page MUST answer the buyer's real questions in plain question-and-answer form (types,
  where each fits, how measuring and installation work, delivery outside Riyadh), with no invented numbers.
- **FR-013**: The site MUST gain a «projects» section of real installations (mosques, gyms, offices) built from
  owner photos, each with location type, product used and date, linked from the relevant product pages.
- **FR-014**: A small guide section MUST answer high-intent questions the clusters raise (e.g. the difference
  between SPC and vinyl-look parquet), each guide linking to its product page.

**Phase D — Local and GEO**

- **FR-015**: Name, address, phone, hours and the legal establishment line MUST be identical across the site,
  structured data, Google Business Profile and directory listings.
- **FR-016**: The site MUST publish a plain-language fact file for AI crawlers (who the shop is, what it sells,
  where, how to contact) and allow the major AI crawlers to read public pages.
- **FR-017**: Key facts MUST be stated as short, quotable sentences near the top of each page and in FAQ form.
- **FR-018**: Business listings MUST be created or corrected on the main Saudi and Arabic directories that AI
  assistants and Google cite.
- **FR-019**: A monthly tracking routine MUST record Search Console clicks/impressions/positions per cluster, the
  20-prompt AI-mention score, and Ads landing-page ratings.

**Rules that apply to all phases**

- **FR-020**: No price, discount, percentage, rating, warranty, years of experience or client count may appear
  unless the owner confirmed it in writing.
- **FR-021**: Changing a page title starts a 21-day lock on that title; every title change is logged with its date.
- **FR-022**: Conversion tracking (WhatsApp and call taps, ad-source marker, second number on parquet/rubber/medical
  pages) MUST keep working after every change and be re-verified on the live site.

### Key Entities

- **Brand mark**: the vector logo in its variants; owner-approved; source of all icons and favicons.
- **Icon set**: the named icons, one family, with usage rules.
- **Product**: name as searched, cluster, target keywords, photos (owner/stock), FAQs, related products.
- **Project**: an installation with type of place, product, city/district, date and owner photos.
- **Photo**: file, product or project, source (owner/stock), checked-for-duplicates flag, faces-free flag.
- **Keyword cluster**: terms, target page, current position, monthly impressions.
- **Business facts**: one record of name, legal line, address, phones, hours, services — the single source for
  pages, structured data and listings.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: 100% of interface icons come from the new family and the brand mark is a vector file approved by the
  owner (checked by an inventory of the site).
- **SC-002**: Products showing stock photos drop from 8 to 3 or fewer within 6 weeks, as owner photos arrive.
- **SC-003**: Google Ads landing-page experience for the parquet, rubber and medical-vinyl keywords moves from
  «below average» to «average» or better within 4 weeks of Phase A and B going live.
- **SC-004**: Organic clicks reach 50 per 28 days within 8 weeks and 120 within 16 weeks (from 8 per 90 days).
- **SC-005**: Brand searches show the site in the top 3; each product cluster has a page in the top 20 within 8
  weeks and the top 10 within 16 weeks.
- **SC-006**: All mobile landing pages reach «good» on Google's three page-experience measures for real visitors.
- **SC-007**: At least 4 of 20 tracked AI-assistant prompts mention the shop within 8 weeks, 8 within 16 weeks.
- **SC-008**: Contacts from the site (WhatsApp + calls) at equal ad spend rise by 20% within 8 weeks, confirmed with
  the owner's own count of real messages.

## Assumptions

- The owner keeps sending real photos on WhatsApp; until a product has them, its stock photos stay labelled.
- The vector logo is a faithful redraw of the existing badge, not a new identity; a new identity would be a separate
  decision and project.
- Icons may come from a professional open-licence family adapted to the palette, or be drawn custom where no
  suitable glyph exists (mosque, carpet roll, rubber roll); both count as «professional SVG».
- The mint/ink/brass palette and the Cairo/Reem Kufi typography approved on 2026-10-27 stay.
- Google Business Profile changes and directory sign-ups are done by the owner or with his login; the plan prepares
  the exact text.
- Horse rubber stays unavailable; its page is not promoted (its fate is a separate owner decision).
- The sister site's playbook (spec 001-seo-rescue in the Khali repo) and the lessons file
  `C:\Users\mohammed\.claude\scripts\SITE-LESSONS.md` are the baseline method.
